Dr. Xingjun Ma

Tenure-track Professor, Fudan Unviersity & SII

I am a faculty member at the Institute of Trustworthy Embodied AI (TEAI), Fudan University, and a full-time supervisor at the Shanghai Innovation Institute. I also serve as an Honorary Fellow at the University of Melbourne. My research focuses on making AI systems safe and trustworthy, with particular interests in foundation models, AI agents, and embodied AI. I am also interested in using AI to advance our understanding of the mind and the universe.
